Flight Finder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06.

What we collect

Nothing. Flight Finder is a static website with no accounts, no database, no cookies and no analytics. The airport codes and dates you enter stay in your browser and are only used to build links.

Third-party sites

When you click a link, you leave this site for Google Flights, Skyscanner or Kayak. Your search details are sent to that site in the link, and their own privacy policies apply.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses under the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

Because we store no personal data, there is nothing to export or delete. Questions: open an issue at github.com/S1xinch/flight-finder.
